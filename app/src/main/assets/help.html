DocLocker Help & FAQ

Welcome to DocLocker — Secure Documents & Smart Conversion workspace.

Secure Document Locker (My Vault)

Your documents are stored locally using SQLite database and app-private encrypted storage. You can import PDFs, images, certificates, and ID cards, view metadata, rename documents, and manage categories.

PDF & File Converter

Convert and manipulate your PDF files easily. Choose from tools like Merge PDF, Split PDF, Compress PDF, and PDF to Word. Converted results can be downloaded to device storage or saved directly into your secure vault.

Privacy Assurance

DocLocker follows a privacy-first approach. No sensitive data or document contents are uploaded to remote servers without explicit user consent.
